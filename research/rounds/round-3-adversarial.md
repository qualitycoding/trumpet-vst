## 5. New questions

1. Before freezing the range table (D-023), should pp at partials ≥ 9 be raised to k ≈ 1.6–2 × threshold, or should the extrapolated thresholds `pth(n) = 4109 + 700 (n − 6)` be re-derived from the *model's* own threshold? 86 and 87 at pp fail because 1.3 × the extrapolated human threshold is below the model threshold.
2. Upward register jumps into p10–p14 arrive but do not persist (D-2). Does Overblow at the top of the range need a different target lip setting, e.g. a higher k, or h0 below the 0.12 mm floor?
3. Should Intonation realism = 1 use the TMM tendencies (p2 valved notes −35 to −62 c)? They conflict with the teaching sources (C-065 slide markings, C-068 +28 c for 1-2-3). A measured valved-trumpet impedance, even one combination, would settle it.
4. How should the T-029 onset tolerance be defined, given that the TinySOL files are trimmed at the attack (D-3)? Measure both signals from a level-relative start, or align on the first −20 dB crossing?
5. Should the shipped YIN estimator be cross-checked against the spike's ACF decisions on the full regime map before the spike numbers are used as acceptance thresholds?
6. Should modes 12–14 (which reuse the mode-11 height ratio) be replaced by a fitted roll-off or by a residual Zc term, given that p12 notes (89; alternates 87 and 88) depend on them?

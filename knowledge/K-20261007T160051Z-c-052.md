Fletcher & Tarnopolsky abstract: threshold blowing pressure approximately proportional to the note frequency; sound output rises ~15 dB per doubling of blowing pressure in an extended region, then ~3 dB per doubling in saturation; some players up to 25 kPa. Measured minimal (threshold) mouth pressures, 4 amateur players, Yamaha YTR1335 (Doc et al. 2023 Table I): written C4 1047 Pa, G4 1788 Pa, C5 2477 Pa, E5 3158 Pa, G5 4109 Pa (registers 2-6, medians). Freour 2020 p. 10: 5 kPa taken as a reasonable upper limit for Bb4 (concert) as measured in trumpet players' mouths (refs 39, 40). Berjamin's simulations used up to 20 kPa.

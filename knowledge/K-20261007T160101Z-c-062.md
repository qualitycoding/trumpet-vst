Yamaha (Tier 1) gives only A3 (3), E4 (3), G4 (1.3), A4 (3) for the 3-valve horn up to A4. Spang and trumpetfingeringchart.com agree on all items listed (Spang labels some 'Alt.', some 'Possible'). Buckner (Tier 2) corroborates the general '1 and 2 makes 3' substitution (esp. high A) and says first-valve-slide helps F5/A5/A#5. C#5 3 (p5) is listed by trumpetfingeringchart.com and generically by Buckner but not on Spang's C#5 card, so it is left out of the consensus. Spang/tfc 'Possible' lists (e.g. G#5=1, B4=13, A#4=123, F5=13, G5=12 or 3) are weaker and listed only in one source each.

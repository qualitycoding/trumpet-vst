Velut: for the saxhorn the model gives a 'ghost note' at fthresh/fac,1 = 1.23 but could NOT reproduce the saxhorn pedal Bb1 (Sec. 4.2-4.3). The ratio fthresh,1/fac,1 increases as fac,1 decreases (Fig. 15). No paper found that simulated the TRUMPET pedal note with this model; Doc 2023 explicitly excludes register 1 ('pedal tone rarely used by trumpet players', Sec. II). Trumpet first resonance is ~83 Hz (Freour 2022 pole 1: 522.47 rad/s) vs the pedal note (written C3, concert Bb2 = 116.5 Hz, UNSW brass page): ratio 1.40 - comparable to the trombone case, so the Velut mechanism plausibly applies (INFERRED, untested). Related: Velut, Vergez, Gilbert 2017 'Physical modelling of trombone mutes, the pedal note issue', Acta Acust. 103(4):668-675; Gilbert & Aumond 2008 'Pedal notes of brass instruments, a mysterious regime of oscillation' (Acoustics'08) - not read.

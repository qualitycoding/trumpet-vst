Harmonic-series cents are exact math: 1200 log2(n) mod 100 rounding. Pedagogical sources (Tier 4) state: 5th and 10th partials flat, 3rd/6th slightly sharp, 7th very flat; 1-3 sharp (use 3rd slide), 1-2-3 very sharp (1st and 3rd slides), 1-2 tends sharp. Valve-combination sharpness, first-order cylinder model (INFERRED, my calculation, valves tuned to ET individually): 1-2 +10 c, 2-3 +15 c, 1-3 +30 c, 1-2-3 +53 c (e.g. 1-2-3: added 0.3712 L vs required 0.4142 L -> 1200 log2(1.4142/1.3712) = +53 c). Real trumpets reduce this by tuning the 3rd loop long. UNSW gives the didjeridu example: after 1+3, the 2nd valve adds only ~3/4 semitone. No peer-reviewed table of measured played-note deviations per partial for a modern B-flat trumpet was accessed (Campbell, Gilbert & Myers 2021 book likely has one; not read).

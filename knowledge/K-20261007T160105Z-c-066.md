Pressbooks: 'the 1st and 2nd valves on their own are naturally slightly flat and the 3rd valve is unusably flat. This is to accommodate fingering combinations with them that become sharper.' JMU: 'All trumpets should have a moveable third valve slide ... for sharp 1-3 and 1-2-3'; Buckner: 'not design flaws, but ... the result of acoustics'. The tsmp.org handout text labels 2-3 'usually flat' and 1-3 and 1-2-3 'usually sharp'.

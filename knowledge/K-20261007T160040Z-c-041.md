Velut 2017: LSA predicts multiple simultaneous instabilities but not which regime wins; e.g. fl = 120 Hz, pb = 6.5 kPa (pthresh 1056 Pa) the model jumps to the 3rd regime at 187.5 Hz (overblowing, Fig. 9 and text pp. 18-19); quasi-periodicity at fl = 110 Hz and period doubling at fl = 55 Hz also occur.

Both from the Yamaha/LMA group (same measurement chain), so not fully independent. Against B-flat equal temperament at A440 (Bb4 = 466.16 Hz) f4 is about -2 to -13 cents. The coordinator's 'typical' list 85, 233, 349, 466, 583, 698, 816, 932 Hz matches Freour 2022 within ~2 Hz for n = 1-5 but Freour's n = 6-8 resonances are lower (690.6, 800.4, 908.0 Hz). UNSW (Wolfe) notes the first resonance is far below the pedal note and that resonances weaken and vanish at high frequency (bell acts as a megaphone above ~700-800 Hz for the bass trombone, i.e. ~1.4-1.6 kHz for the half-scale trumpet - INFERRED scaling).

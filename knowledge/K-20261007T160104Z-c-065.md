TSMP handout text: for 1-3 / 1-2-3 'Lip down. Kick first valve slide. Use 3rd valve, kick 3rd slide' and 'Learn KICK OUT 3rd valve slide as normal fingering' (text only; notated examples are images I did not see). Buckner adds: when trilling low D-Eb, correct D with the first slide so Eb stays in tune (no air goes through the 1st slide on Eb); for D-E either slide works. The question's guess 'first slide for D5 (1), C#5 (12), E4 (12)' is only partly supported: E4(12) yes (Spang), C#5 and D5 not annotated by Spang; Buckner only generically allows it for valve-1 notes.

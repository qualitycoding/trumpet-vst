Numbers are my calculation (INFERRED): L_eff = c/(2 x 116.54 Hz) = 1.472 m at c = 343 m/s. Physical loop lengths differ from these because the effective length includes mouthpiece/bell end corrections and the loops are inserted in the ~11.6 mm cylindrical section; recommended practice: in the offline TMM, insert cylindrical loops (r = 5.82 mm in Tr_co_MP) at x ~ 0.35-0.75 m and tune each loop length so that resonances 2-8 shift by the intended interval, then optionally lengthen the 3rd loop (and 1st-valve trigger) as makers do. OpenWInD supports valves natively (InstrumentGeometry with 'valve' rows: position, reconnection, radius, length; fingering chart 'o'/'x'), see examples/technical/Ex2bis_Brass_Valves.py (example values there are arbitrary, not a real trumpet). Harrison, Bilbao & Perry DAFx-15 used a 1.381 m trumpet with one test valve (default 0.02 m, bypass 0.2 m) - not a real valve set. | R3: superseded for design purposes by the TMM-tuned loops (planner spike, make_table.py); no measured loop lengths are needed (D-004).

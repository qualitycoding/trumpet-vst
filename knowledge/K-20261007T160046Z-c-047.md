L_NDeq = int_0^L Dmin/D(x) dx (Campbell 2020 eq. 4). L_NDeq ~ 0.85 m is INFERRED (0.58 x 1.47). OpenWInD value computed by me from Tr_co_MP integrating from the leadpipe start (x = 0.0837 m), excluding the mouthpiece; c = 343 m/s, f1 = 116.54 Hz. So the plan's guess of 1.3-1.5 m of cylinder is too long: use ~0.85-0.90 m of ~9 mm-diameter equivalent cylinder (the geometric cylindrical section alone is ~0.5 m in Tr_co_MP). Campbell 2020 also: E1 = 1 + alpha B / Dmin with alpha = 5 mm (eq. 6) as spectral-enrichment predictor.

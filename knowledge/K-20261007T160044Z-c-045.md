Generalized Burgers (Gilbert 2008 eqs. 1-2, dimensionless): dq+/dsigma - q+ dq+/dtheta+ + q+ d ln(D/D0)/dsigma = (1/Gamma) d2q+/dtheta2 - (T/eps) d^(1/2) q+/dtheta^(1/2); sigma = ((gamma+1)/2) M x (slow scale). Backward wave has twin equation; forward/backward waves do not interact in the bulk (only at ends). Berjamin et al. (Menguy-Gilbert model, eq. 3a-4): du+/dt + d/dx(a u+ + b u+^2/2) + (a/S) dS/dx u+ = -c d^(-1/2)/dt^(-1/2) du+/dx + d d2u+/dx2, a = a0, b = (gamma+1)/2, c(x) = C a0 sqrt(nu)/R(x), d = nu_d/2. Gilbert 2007 Fig.1 example: cylinder r = 6 mm, sine 350 Hz, 5000 Pa -> shock formation distance 3.64 m.

Buckner: 'extended about one-half inch ... 1-3 and about one inch ... 1-2-3'; 'only notes normally fingered 1-3 are low D and low G ... 1-2-3 are low C-sharp and low F-sharp'. AmericanFork p.114: 1-3 'very sharp - you must use the third valve slide', 1-2-3 'extremely sharp'. Gillis: '1&3 and 1,2&3 sharp (1-2-3 even more) ... third valve slide needs to be lengthened'. Same statements repeated by promusicvault and thewholehorn (Tier 4).

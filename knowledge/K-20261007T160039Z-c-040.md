Freour p.10: 'As expected for an outward-striking lip model, the lip natural frequency is smaller than the oscillation frequency of the corresponding regime.' Contrast with Doc 2023 Table II (Q_l = 20): fl = 235, 340, 467, 586, 703 Hz for registers 2-6, i.e. fl ~ 1.0 x f_ac,n (resonances ~ 233, 349, 463, 582, 691 Hz from Freour 2022 Table 1 trumpet), and Doc Fig.7 text: minimum-threshold point has fthresh close to fl. Rule for implementation: the fl/f_ac ratio depends strongly on Q_l: ~0.82 for Q_l = 3, ~0.93-0.98 for Q_l = 7, ~1.0 for Q_l = 20. Use a per-regime lookup tuned by LSA offline.

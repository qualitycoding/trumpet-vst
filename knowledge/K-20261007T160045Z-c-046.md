gamma P_at = rho0 c0^2, so both forms are identical. Numerical examples are my calculations (INFERRED). Hirschberg: shocks expected in the trombone at ff because its cylindrical segment (2-3 m) exceeds x_s; steep mouthpiece pressure rise at lip opening sets (dp/dt)_max; '...we can also expect that non-linear wave propagation is musically relevant for the trumpet'. Proposed real-time caricature (Hirschberg p. 4): linear model of pipe with >= 8 resonances drives lips; computed mouthpiece pressure drives a simple wave into a cylindrical bore (weak-shock theory if needed); output radiated through a linear horn model with audio-band filtering. This is exactly the plan's 'output-side nonlinear propagation' architecture.
